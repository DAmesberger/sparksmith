Use {key:app.help} with a control focused or under the pointer to open its help. Type a term such as “annular ring” in the search field. Pick a result in the relevant group to read its facts and follow related topics.

Use the arrows and Enter to open a search result. Press {key:help.back} to revisit the previous page or {key:help.forward} to return. In the desktop reader, a key label follows your personal binding.

Findings explain the cause and the suggested fix. Rules give the value and its source. To learn a word used in a card, search for its glossary entry, for example [[term/footprint]]. To see a command’s contract in automation, use [[entry/project.schema]].
