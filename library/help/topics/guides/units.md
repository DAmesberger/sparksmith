Sparksmith shows lengths in millimetres. It cannot be switched to inches or mil, and Settings has no choice of units, language or theme.

- A dimension and a parameter take an inch value with its unit, such as 1 in or 100 mil.
- A field with mm after its number, such as a position, takes millimetres only. 1 inch is 25.4 mm, and 1 mil is 0.0254 mm.
- A fab profile's rule can be read in another unit: open the rule in Help and choose Length unit. It changes that page only.
- A part's size code such as 0603 reads as the inch code. 0603M, or “metric 0603”, names the metric one.
- The window follows the light or dark setting of your system. The language is English.
