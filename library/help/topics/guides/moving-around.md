- Turn the wheel to zoom at the pointer. On a trackpad, pinch.
- Drag with the middle mouse button to move the view. On a trackpad, use two fingers.
- Press {key:view.fit} to see all of it.
- In 3D, drag with the right button to turn the board.
- Rest the pointer on a part, pin, pad, track, via or wire: a small card beside it says what it is, such as a part's number or value and its package. It works on the sheet, the board and in 3D. Press Esc to close it. With the keyboard on the view, the selected thing's card shows.
- In Layout, {key:pcb.layer-focus} dims the other layers, and [[command/pcb.from-bottom]] looks from below.
