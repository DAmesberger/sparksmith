The help search's embedding model, library/help/model, is potion-base-8M by
the Minish Lab (huggingface.co/minishlab/potion-base-8M, revision
bf8b056651a2c21b8d2565580b8569da283cab23, its model card: licence MIT),
distilled from BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5 (MIT). tokenizer.json and config.json
are its files; model.safetensors holds its weights as 8-bit integers, made
by crates/help-search/examples/quantize.rs from the file with SHA-256
f65d0f325faadc1e121c319e2faa41170d3fa07d8c89abd48ca5358d9a223de2.
